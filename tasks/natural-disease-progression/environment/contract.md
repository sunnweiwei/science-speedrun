# Environment contract

This track evaluates autonomous neural architecture development on a fixed longitudinal risk task. The final inference graph must be one jointly trained neural model that learns patient representations by gradient optimization. Neural branches, experts, modality encoders, disease heads, and learned temporal decoders are allowed. Tree models, random forests, stratified/rate-table predictors, fixed population time scaling, and ensembles containing non-neural predictors are not permitted in final predictions. Automated verification checks packaging and outputs; compliance with this architecture rule is established by reviewing the submitted training and inference path.

Provide an executable `infer.sh INPUT_DIR OUTPUT_DIR`. The frozen horizons are `30,60,...,360,365` days. Return cumulative first-record disease probabilities with shape `[batch,num_scored_diseases,13]` and death probabilities with shape `[batch,13]`. The model must learn patient-specific multi-horizon predictions rather than multiplying one endpoint risk by a fixed population curve. Values must be finite, in `[0,1]`, and nondecreasing over time. The required parquet contains only `sample_id`, flattened disease-major `disease_risk`, and `death_risk`.

The input contains cutoff-bounded samples and structured events. Training additionally supplies labels and validity masks; validation truth is available only through the local scorer, and hidden test truth remains in the independent grader. `sample_id` is an alignment key, never a feature. See `data/README.md` for the exact visible interface.

The reward is `panel_recall_at_5pct` (`evaluation/panel_scoring.py`), computed from the 365-day disease predictions. For each endpoint of the prespecified panel (`PANEL`: heart failure, atrial fibrillation, acute MI, ischaemic stroke, intracerebral haemorrhage, pulmonary embolism, venous thrombosis, aortic aneurysm/dissection, type 2 diabetes, CKD, AKI, sepsis, pneumonia, respiratory failure, cirrhosis, hepatic failure, dementia, COPD, interstitial lung disease, colon/liver/pancreas/prostate/bladder cancer and two metastasis groups), eligible patients are complete-year patients with a valid 365-day label whose diagnosis history contains none of the endpoint's precursor or recoding codes. They are ranked by predicted risk; the endpoint score is the share of its 365-day cases among the top 5 % (ceil(0.05 n)), with ties credited at their expectation, so a constant prediction scores ceil(0.05 n)/n ≈ 0.05 (0.0501 on the test set). The reward is the mean over endpoints with at least 20 eligible cases; that set depends only on the frozen labels. Validation and test samples are ICD-10-era index admissions.

Also reported, not rewarded: per-endpoint recall at 1 %, PPV, AUROC, AUPRC and calibration (prediction/observed ratio, calibration slope, decile ECE); disease-first macro time-averaged AUROC over all diseases (the earlier primary metric); AUPRC; raw and frozen-normalized Brier; per-time AUROC; death metrics; and age/sex strata. Invalid predictions score 0.

The submission must also include the model definition and actual inference code, training entry point, frozen configuration, trained weights, preprocessing/vocabulary artifacts, and `README.md`. These artifacts support reproducibility and architecture review; filenames and declarations are not substitutes for reviewing what `infer.sh` executes. The package may contain only ordinary files and directories (no symlinks; hard links are fine), at most 50 GiB in total.
