RFD3 enzyme (rigid-ligand active sites, upstream mcsa_41)

Budget, as this task runs it:
    100 backbones per target, 4 sequences per backbone, 5 predictor models per sequence.

Criteria. The backbone passes if BOTH hold:
1. Motif sidechain recapitulation. ANY of the 5 predictor models for ANY of the 4
   LigandMPNN sequences has, after aligning on the motif backbone (N, CA, C), a motif
   all-atom RMSD <= 1.5 A.
2. No ligand clashes. A ligand clash is any non-motif backbone atom (N, CA or C) within
   1.5 A of the closest ligand atom.

Criterion 2 is measured on the raw backbone, so the grader checks it BEFORE folding -
purely to skip a fold that cannot change the verdict, never as an extra requirement. It
rejected 60% of enzyme designs on the 2026-09-28 reference run (2,475 of 4,100), which is
most of the grading cost this track would otherwise spend on designs that cannot pass.
Nothing else runs before folding except contract validation of the request: `motif_map`
must name one distinct in-range residue per motif position carrying the requested
identity, or criterion 1 has nothing to measure against.

Removed 2026-09-26: this file previously also described conditioning gates applied before
folding - motif all-atom RMSD on the raw design, and ligand burial (>= 50% of ligand
atoms within 5 A). Neither is in `success.txt`'s criteria, both were this task's own
invention, and together with the other pre-fold gates they discarded 59% of DESIGNABLE
enzyme designs (0.022 against 0.054). They are gone from the grader; the pass rate is now
the published criteria alone.

Deviations from RFdiffusion3's published protocol, deliberate and recorded:
  - 4 sequences per backbone, not 8. Uniform across all four tracks; "pass if ANY
    sequence passes" means this can only lower a pass rate, never raise it.
  - RoseTTAFold3 is the predictor, not Chai-1/AlphaFold. Parameters for RF3 are public
    and ship in the image; the published thresholds were calibrated with AlphaFold, so
    these numbers are internally comparable but NOT comparable with the RFD3 paper.
