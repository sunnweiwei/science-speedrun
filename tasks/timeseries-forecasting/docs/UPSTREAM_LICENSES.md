## The corpus card's licence does not describe all of its constituents

`Salesforce/GiftEvalPretrain` is tagged Apache-2.0, but it is an aggregation of
public datasets that keep their own terms, and at least one of them is
non-commercial. The task does not redistribute any of it: `materialize.py`
downloads from the pinned upstream revisions, so the terms that bind a user are
the constituents' own.

| directory or group | share of the allow-list | licence | verified |
|---|---:|---|---|
| `largest_2017` … `largest_2021` (LargeST) | 17.81 GB, **43.1%** | **CC BY-NC 4.0** ("The LargeST benchmark dataset is released under a CC BY-NC 4.0 International License"; the code is MIT) | yes, [github.com/liuxu77/LargeST](https://github.com/liuxu77/LargeST) read 2026-09-18 |
| `extended_web_traffic_with_missing`, `kaggle_web_traffic_weekly` | 1.56 GB, 3.8% | **CC BY 4.0** — these reached the corpus through the Monash archive, whose "intended for research purpose" note is a request, not a licence term | yes, [Monash-University/monash_tsf](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Monash-University/monash_tsf) read 2026-09-20 |
| `m5`, `favorita_sales`, `favorita_transactions`, `godaddy` | 0.80 GB, 1.9% | Kaggle competition terms, **unresolved**: the rules pages need a login, so the redistribution clause has not been read | no — see `docs/validation/republication_readiness.json` |
| `Q-TRAFFIC`, `alibaba_cluster_trace_2018`, `china_air_quality`, `kdd2022`, `ideal` | 4.44 GB, 10.7% | research-use or no stated licence | reported by survey, not individually re-read |
| `PEMS03/04/07/08`, `PEMS_BAY`, `LOS_LOOP` | 0.33 GB, 0.8% | PeMS registration required | reported by survey, not individually re-read |
| remainder (Azure VM traces, residential load and PV, wind farms, London smart meters, Monash-derived sets, …) | ~16.4 GB, ~39.7% | CC BY 4.0 or permissive | partially verified |

What follows for this task: it is a **research** benchmark, and a model trained
on the corpus inherits the non-commercial restriction of LargeST unless that
directory is excluded. Salesforce itself took this position one layer up, where
every Moirai checkpoint trained on this corpus is released `cc-by-nc-4.0` while
the data card says Apache-2.0. Two clean options, for the maintainers to pick:

1. Keep the allow-list as it is, and state that submissions are research
   artifacts under non-commercial terms. This is the status quo and matches
   what the upstream model authors did.
2. Drop `largest_*` and the Kaggle-derived directories, which costs 20.2 GB of
   41.3 GB and about 63% of the corpus's scalar values, leaving a corpus whose
   redistribution terms are unambiguous.

The task's own code, documents and the synthetic generator in
`environment/baseline/starter/synth.py` are unaffected either way.
